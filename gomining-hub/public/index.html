Uses GoMining's latest daily payout (settled once a day) and fees, plus your own miner and discounts

Which discounts, prices and payouts to enter?
  • Total maintenance discount is the combined discount on electricity + service your GoMining app shows for your miners. It adds up paying maintenance in GOMINING (up to 20%), your VIP level (0% to 6%) and the Service Button (3%). Enter the total from your app, or work it out with Build your discount below.
  • Electricity defaults to the rate in GoMining's latest payout data. Enter a different $/kWh if your miners use another rate.
  • Price per TH, new purchases defaults to GoMining's listed price for new miners (primary market, not the secondary marketplace).
  • Reinvesting into an existing miner is priced as a power upgrade, not as a new miner. Enter the power-upgrade price per TH your app shows, and any reinvest bonus (for example a VIP +5% or +10%). GoMining's reinvestment eligibility rules and minimums aren't checked here.
  • 365-day average is GoMining's total gross payout per TH over the last year divided by 365, which smooths out daily swings.
  • BTC price scenarios keep your sats per TH the same (set by network difficulty, not price) and value them at the chosen price; electricity and service fees stay in USD. Difficulty is held constant, although in reality it tends to rise when BTC does.
  • Miner Wars rewards aren't included here: see HONKSQUAD in Miner Wars for the clan's numbers and a Miner Wars vs plain mining comparison.

How it works

Where the numbers come from. GoMining publishes the daily payout per TH, the BTC price it uses, the electricity and service fees and its miner prices. This site reads them live every few minutes, adds Bitcoin network data from mempool.space and prices from CoinGecko, and does the maths.

Net reward = payout − electricity (your W/TH × the electricity rate) − service fee, per TH per day. Your total maintenance discount (mining-mode, service-button, GOMINING payment and VIP discounts, as your app shows them) reduces electricity + service by that percentage. GoMining settles the payout once a day, so the numbers change daily, not live.

My miners stays in your own browser. Clearing your browser data or switching device starts it empty again. Your GoMining account is never connected or asked for.

Estimates, not promises. Everything assumes the latest payout and fees stay the same, and the calculators don't include Miner Wars rewards. BTC price, network difficulty and GoMining's fees change, so treat payback and plans as a guide. Not financial advice.

HONKSQUAD in Miner Wars reads GoMining's public Miner Wars leaderboards (the same ones the app shows): the clan board every 10 minutes and the member list every 30. The clan's BTC is estimated as blocks won × the league's BTC per block (prize fund ÷ blocks mined so far), and shared by TH. Spells, GOMINING rewards and GoMining's own adjustments aren't included.

Outlook: break-even BTC price = electricity + service per TH ÷ BTC paid per TH. The halving countdown uses the current block height and average block time from mempool.space. Fear & Greed comes from alternative.me.
