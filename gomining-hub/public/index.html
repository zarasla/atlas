Which discounts, prices and payouts to enter?
  • Total maintenance discount is the combined discount on electricity + service your GoMining app shows for your miners. It can include an automatic mining-mode discount, the service-button discount, paying maintenance in GOMINING and your VIP level. These change and depend on your account, so this site can't know them: enter your own total.
  • Electricity defaults to the rate in GoMining's latest payout data. Enter a different $/kWh if your miners use another rate.
  • Price per TH, new purchases defaults to GoMining's listed price for new miners (primary market, not the secondary marketplace).
  • Reinvesting into an existing miner is priced as a power upgrade, not as a new miner. Enter the power-upgrade price per TH your app shows, and any reinvest bonus (for example a VIP +5% or +10%). GoMining's reinvestment eligibility rules and minimums aren't checked here.
  • 365-day average is GoMining's total gross payout per TH over the last year divided by 365, which smooths out daily swings.
  • Miner Wars rewards aren't included anywhere in these calculators.

How it works

Where the numbers come from. GoMining publishes the daily payout per TH, the BTC price it uses, the electricity and service fees and its miner prices. This site reads them live every few minutes, adds Bitcoin network data from mempool.space and prices from CoinGecko, and does the maths.

Net reward = payout − electricity (your W/TH × the electricity rate) − service fee, per TH per day. Your total maintenance discount (mining-mode, service-button, GOMINING payment and VIP discounts, as your app shows them) reduces electricity + service by that percentage. GoMining settles the payout once a day, so the numbers change daily, not live.

My miners stays in your own browser. Clearing your browser data or switching device starts it empty again. Your GoMining account is never connected or asked for.

Estimates, not promises. Everything assumes the latest payout and fees stay the same, and Miner Wars rewards are not included. BTC price, network difficulty and GoMining's fees change, so treat payback and plans as a guide. Not financial advice.
