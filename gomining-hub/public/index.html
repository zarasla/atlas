Which discounts, prices and payouts to enter?
  • Total maintenance discount is the combined discount on electricity + service your GoMining app shows for your miners (Rewards → Maintenance). It adds up paying maintenance in GOMINING (up to 20%), your VIP level (0% to 6%) and the Service Button (0.3% a day pressed in a row, up to 3%), 29% together at most, plus the Mining mode discount, which GoMining changes every week with the veGOMINING vote. Enter the total from your app, or work it out with Build your discount below. Discounts don't apply to the Bonus Miner.
  • Electricity defaults to the rate in GoMining's latest payout data. Enter a different $/kWh if your miners use another rate.
  • Price per TH, new purchases defaults to GoMining's listed price for new miners (primary market, not the secondary marketplace).
  • Reinvesting into an existing miner is priced as a power upgrade, not as a new miner. Enter the power-upgrade price per TH your app shows, and your VIP reinvest bonus (+5% from Silver I, +10% from Diamond I). The plan reinvests day by day under GoMining's rules: the miner needs 10 to 5,000 TH and better than 20 W/TH, and a day's reward must be at least $0.10, or it's paid in BTC instead.
  • 365-day average is GoMining's total gross payout per TH over the last year divided by 365, which smooths out daily swings.
  • BTC price scenarios keep your sats per TH the same (set by network difficulty, not price) and value them at the chosen price; electricity and service fees stay in USD. Difficulty is held constant, although in reality it tends to rise when BTC does.
  • Pre-halving. Every figure uses today's payout. The next halving (about April 2028) halves the BTC paid per TH; where BTC's price goes then can't be predicted, so it isn't modelled. Paybacks that run past it are estimates at today's rates only.
  • Miner Wars rewards aren't included here: the calculators cover Mining mode.

Your VIP level

From your mining power or your veGOMINING votes, whichever is higher (GoMining's official VIP table)

Only power you created or bought from GoMining counts (miners bought from other users after 15 Aug 2025 and the Bonus Miner don't). veGOMINING votes fall every week, so a level held by votes can slip. Referral activity over the last 180 days is a third path; GoMining shows its thresholds only in the app.

Simple Earn

Idle balances earn BTC, paid every 4 hours: base APR × your VIP multiplier

GoMining sets each asset's APR and changes it often, and shows the current one only in your wallet (Wallet → Simple Earn), so type it in; this site never assumes a rate. Rewards are counted on the lowest balance in each full 4-hour cycle and paid in BTC, or in TH with 10% more TH when a cycle earns at least $0.10. Needs KYC Basic and isn't offered in some countries, including the United States.

Simple Earn or mining?

The same money kept in Simple Earn (paid in BTC every 4 hours, or in TH), or spent on a new GoMining miner (paid once a day)

Simple Earn counts the lowest balance in each full 4-hour cycle (00:00, 04:00, … UTC) and pays in BTC, or in TH with 10% more TH when a cycle earns at least $0.10; the TH are added once a day to a miner of 20 W/TH or better. Your capital stays yours and can be withdrawn any time. The miner is bought at GoMining's list price per TH and pays its net reward once a day after your discount (never below zero, GoMining's reward protection); you keep it, but what it would sell for later can't be predicted, so it isn't counted. All at today's payout, fees and BTC price, before the halving.

Burn & Mint

Every Tuesday GoMining burns the GOMINING paid for maintenance and mints fewer back

Loading the Burn & Mint record…

Minted = (1 − V × (1 − C)) × burned, where C is the epoch's mint coefficient and V the share of veGOMINING votes for burning; V is read back from each week's numbers. New tokens go 65% to hashpower providers, 20% to veGOMINING holders, 10% to GOMINING rewards (Mining mode discount, Odyssey prizes, Greedy Machines) and 5% to the team.

veGOMINING lock

Votes, VIP level and rewards from locking GOMINING

Votes = tokens × lock days ÷ 1,461 (4 years), falling each week to zero when the lock ends; locked tokens can't be withdrawn early. Rewards use GoMining's current yearly income per vote, which moves every week with the Burn & Mint cycle, so treat them as an estimate. Votes count towards your VIP level, and the locked tokens count, with your wallet balance, towards the GOMINING maintenance discount.

Your own stats in Claude

GoMining's official MCP connector reads your account, read-only

  1. Sign in to app.gomining.com in your browser.
  2. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors, press + and choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it GoMining and paste the server URL: https://mcp.gomining.com/mcp
  4. Press Connect and approve access on GoMining's consent screen.

Then ask things like:

  • “What did my miners earn this month, day by day?”
  • “What's my VIP level and how far is the next one?”
  • “Show my Simple Earn rates and rewards.”
  • “Which of my miners should I upgrade first?”

The connector is GoMining's own (docs.gomining.com → GoMining AI → MCP Server). It works in Claude, Claude Code, Cursor and other MCP apps, is read-only (it can't move funds or change settings) and never passes through this site. Answers are AI-generated, so double-check anything important in the app.

How it works

Where the numbers come from. Only from GoMining itself and public sources: GoMining's live public API (daily payout per TH, the BTC rate it used, electricity and service fees, miner prices, upgrade prices, Burn & Mint, veGOMINING and platform statistics), GoMining's documentation and official VIP table for the rules, mempool.space for the Bitcoin network and CoinGecko for prices. Anything GoMining shows only inside your account (your Mining mode discount, your Simple Earn APRs) you type in yourself: the site never guesses it.

Net reward = payout − (electricity + service) × (1 − discounts), per TH per day, GoMining's own formula. Electricity is your W/TH × GoMining's rate per kWh × 24 h. Your total maintenance discount (GOMINING payment up to 20%, VIP up to 6%, Service Button up to 3%, plus the weekly Mining mode discount) reduces electricity + service by that percentage. GoMining settles the payout once a day, so the numbers change daily, not live.

Pre-halving only. Every estimate uses today's payout. The next halving (about April 2028) halves the BTC paid per TH, and nobody can say what BTC's price will do then, so it isn't modelled.

My miners stays in your own browser. Clearing your browser data or switching device starts it empty again. Your GoMining account is never connected or asked for.

Estimates, not promises. Everything assumes the latest payout and fees stay the same, and the calculators don't include Miner Wars rewards. BTC price, network difficulty and GoMining's fees change, so treat payback and plans as a guide. Not financial advice.

GOMINING token: Burn & Mint comes from GoMining's public record of every weekly cycle, epochs and mint coefficients from GoMining's tokenomics docs, veGOMINING totals and the yearly income per vote from GoMining's lock statistics. Votes = tokens × lock days ÷ 1,461, which matches GoMining's own totals.

Your own account isn't connected to this site. For your real balances, rewards and rates, use GoMining's official MCP connector in Claude (see Platform).

Outlook: break-even BTC price = electricity + service per TH ÷ BTC paid per TH, at today's difficulty. The halving countdown uses the current block height from mempool.space and 10-minute blocks (the pace difficulty adjustments aim for). Fear & Greed comes from alternative.me.
